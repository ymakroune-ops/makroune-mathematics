A little about me

Mathematics, in
teaching and research.

A space for sharing mathematical ideas, academic work, and resources for students and fellow researchers.

“Mathematics is a way of seeing structure, asking precise questions, and discovering unexpected connections.”

I am a mathematics teacher and researcher with an academic background in pure mathematics and differential geometry. My work brings together the practice of teaching mathematics and the study of geometric structures on complex manifolds.

My research focuses particularly on locally conformally Kähler (LCK) geometry, including Vaisman manifolds, curvature, and geometric structures associated with Hermitian manifolds.

Research

Questions in geometry.

My research explores Hermitian geometry and the interplay between curvature, complex structures, and global geometric properties.

LCK Geometry

Locally conformally Kähler manifolds, Lee forms, conformal changes, and geometric structures that are locally Kähler.

Current directions

Themes I explore

These themes connect my current work with broader questions in complex and differential geometry.

01

Curvature and geometric structures

Understanding how curvature conditions constrain the geometry and topology of complex manifolds.

02

Harmonic maps

Variational aspects of maps between Hermitian manifolds, including tension and bitension fields.

03

Exact LCK structures

Morse–Novikov cohomology, twisted differentials, and special classes of locally conformally Kähler manifolds.
